    Respectful Wearing Guidelines

    • Learn the event context before choosing ceremonial pieces.
    • Avoid mixing sacred motifs casually or combining elements from unrelated traditions.
    • When uncertain, consult a tailor or cultural advisor from the Buryat community.
    • Traditional garments are heirlooms — store them carefully and repair rather than replace.
